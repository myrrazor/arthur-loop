Privacy

Privacy, in plain English.

The Arthur Loop site does not follow you around. The software keeps its working state on the machine and in the directory you choose.

Updated September 15, 2026

What this site collects

Nothing through the page itself. There are no accounts, forms, analytics, advertising pixels, or visitor profiles. The site sets no cookies and does not put tracking data in local storage.

The site is served by Vercel. Hosting infrastructure receives connection information, such as an IP address and the requested URL, and may retain request logs under its own policies. The page itself does not load analytics or advertising scripts.

Contact

Questions and privacy requests go through GitHub issues or security advisories. This site does not publish a personal email address and does not operate a support inbox.

Your Arthur Loop files

Queues, artifacts, decisions, sessions, and quota snapshots stay in the instance directory you control. The marketing site cannot see them. Delete the directory and any backups you made when you want that state gone.

Adapters may send data to tools you configure. Those tools have their own storage and privacy rules, so remove data there separately.

Links that leave this site

GitHub and other external destinations operate under their own privacy terms. Arthur Loop does not create an account when you follow those links.
